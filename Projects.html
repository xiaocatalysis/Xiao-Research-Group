Current Research

I investigate topics in biomass & shale gas conversion, and chemical & catalytic reaction engineering. The projects typically involve combined experimental and modeling studies. A few representative projects are as follows.

  • Oxidative coupling of methane
  • Catalytic bio-oil upgrading
  • Catalytic conversion of glycerol to high-value chemicals and liquid fuels
  • Density functional theory (DFT) calculations for Pt-Bi bimetallic catalysts
  • The bench-scale biodiesel production via heterogeneous catalytic process

Previous Research

I initially started my research life in 2009-2010 as a senior undergraduate, by developing an Eley-Rideal kinetic model for biodiesel production in a fixed-bed reactor. Using this work, I published my first paper (Energy Fuels, 2010, 24 (11), pp 5829-5833) as the leading author. Then I continually work on the biodiesel project in 2010-2012, after I joined Dr. Guomin Xiao's research group at Southeast University (China) as a Ph. D. student. As the primary participant, I designed a bench scale setup for catalytic production of biodiesel, equipped with home-made pellet catalysts and PLC (programmable logic controllers). The setup has a capacity of 50 lb per day and a catalyst life of more than 1,500 hours along with biodiesel yield larger than 90%. My second paper (Ind. Eng. Chem. Res., 2012, 51 (37), pp 11860-11865) was published on the basis of macro-kinetics and reactor modeling for the bench-scale reactor. Since Fall of 2012 when I joined Dr. Varma's research group at Purdue University, I had been focusing on utilization of glycerol, as the byproduct of biodiesel. I developed a universal purification method to recycle crude glycerol (Ind. Eng. Chem. Res., 2013, 52 (39), pp 14291-14296).
