Biography

Dr. Yang Jason Xiao earned his B.S. in Chemical Engineering from Southeast University (Nanjing, China) in 2010. He completed his Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering through Purdue University/Southeast University in 2015. His doctoral research was co-advised by Prof. Guomin Xiao at Southeast University (2010 - 2012) and Prof. Arvind Varma at Purdue University (2012 - 2015).

Following his Ph.D., Dr. Xiao served as a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Purdue University (2015 - 2018), conducting catalytic reaction engineering research with Prof. Arvind Varma and computational catalysis research with Prof. Jeffrey P. Greeley. He subsequently served as a Research Scientist (2018 - 2019) and Senior Research Engineer (2019 - 2022) at Purdue University. In 2022, Dr. Xiao joined Louisiana Tech University as a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Chemical Engineering, where he is Principal Investigator of the Reaction Engineering and Catalysis Science Laboratory (RECSL).
