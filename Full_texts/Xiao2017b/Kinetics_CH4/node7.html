Kinetics Measurements

The kinetics measurements were conducted in a fixed-bed reactor setup, described in our prior work. [#Gao2014##1###,#Gao2015##1###] Prior to reaction, the packed catalyst was activated at 400 #tex2html_wrap_inline384#C and 1 atm for 4 hours under a gas mixture flow (H#tex2html_wrap_inline386#:N#tex2html_wrap_inline388# = 1:2). The reactor was then purged using N#tex2html_wrap_inline390# for 30 min. The standard reactor operating conditions were: 400 #tex2html_wrap_inline392#C, 1 atm, 0.02-0.05 g catalyst for differential reactions (conversions typically ;SPMlt; 6-8) and 0.3-0.5 g catalyst for integral reactions (conversions typically ;SPMgt; 20), total gas (CH#tex2html_wrap_inline394#:N#tex2html_wrap_inline396# = 1:1) flow rate 100 mL/min, and guaiacol feed rate 0.025 mL/min (liquid, at room temperature).

The feed flow rates correspond to a molar ratio of 10 between CH#tex2html_wrap_inline398# and guaiacol. Blank tests of AC support with no metal loading were conducted under the standard reaction conditions, and guaiacol conversion was less than 1. Without guaiacol, methane exhibited about 5 conversion, generating H#tex2html_wrap_inline400# and trace C#tex2html_wrap_inline402#H#tex2html_wrap_inline404# on Pt-Bi catalyst under the standard conditions. All experiments have carbon mass balances of 92 #tex2html_wrap_inline406# 2, similar to the literature. [#Chang2013##1###,#Jongerius2013##1###] Possible factors affecting mass balance include liquid hold-up in various locations in the system, particularly the condenser, and coke deposit on the catalyst.
